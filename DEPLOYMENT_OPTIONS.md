# 🆓 FREE Deployment Options

Deploy your AI Code Tutor platform **completely FREE**!

## Option 1: Render + Vercel (Recommended FREE)

**Cost**: $0/month
**Setup Time**: 15 minutes

### What You Get
- ✅ Backend on Render (free tier)
- ✅ PostgreSQL database (free, 1GB)
- ✅ Redis on Upstash (free, 10k commands/day)
- ✅ Frontend on Vercel (free, unlimited)

### Trade-offs
- ⚠️ Backend "sleeps" after 15 mins inactivity
- ⚠️ First request after sleep: 30-60s wake-up time
- ✅ Perfect for: Portfolio, testing, demos, low-traffic apps
